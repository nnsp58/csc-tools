Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many images can I combine?

A: You can upload and combine up to 50 images at once. For best performance, we recommend batches of 20-30 images.

Q: Is my data safe?

A: Yes! All image-to-PDF conversion happens directly in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to our servers.

Q: Can I reorder images?

A: Absolutely. After uploading, you'll see "Up" and "Down" buttons next to each image to organize them exactly how you want.
