Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the standard passport photo size?

A: Our tool uses the international standard ratio (3.5 x 4.5 cm). The final sheet is optimized for high-quality photo paper printing.

Q: Does this tool remove the background?

A: Currently, the tool overlays your photo on the selected background color. For best results, we recommend using a photo with a plain white or light blue background.

Q: Is my privacy protected?

A: Absolutely. All image processing happens locally in your browser. Your face data is never uploaded to any server.
