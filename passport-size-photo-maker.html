Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the standard passport photo size?

A: India standard is 3.5 x 4.5 cm. Our tool uses the correct ratio for all major standards.

Q: Can I use Joint (couple) photos?

A: Yes! Select "Joint Passport (Landscape)" or click the "Landscape" button while cropping.

Q: Is my photo secure?

A: 100% secure. All processing happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
