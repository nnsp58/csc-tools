Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does this tool upload my audio to a server?

A: No. All audio processing happens locally in your browser using the Web Audio API. Your data stays on your machine.

Q: What is the difference between MP3 and WAV?

A: MP3 is a compressed format that saves space but loses some quality. WAV is an uncompressed, lossless format that maintains full audio quality but results in much larger file sizes.

Q: Why is my WAV file so much bigger than my MP3?

A: WAV files store every single audio sample without compression. A typical 3-minute song can be 30MB in WAV format, compared to just 3MB in MP3.
