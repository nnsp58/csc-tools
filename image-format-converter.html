Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which format is best for the web?

A: WebP is highly recommended for web use as it offers superior compression and quality, making your website load faster.

Q: Will I lose quality when converting?

A: When converting to JPEG, there is a minor quality loss due to compression (we use a high-quality 95% setting). Converting between PNG and WebP is generally lossless.

Q: Is my image data safe?

A: Yes. All conversion happens locally in your browser. Your images are never sent to our servers.
