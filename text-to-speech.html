Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Text to Speech kya hai?

A: Text to Speech (TTS) ek technology hai jo written text ko bolne wali awaaz mein convert karti hai. Yeh tool browser ki built-in speech synthesis ka use karta hai.

Q: Speech to Text kya hai?

A: Speech to Text (STT) ek technology hai jo aapki bolne wali baat ko automatically written text mein convert kar deti hai â€” bilkul dictation jaisi. Yeh Web Speech API (Chrome/Edge) use karta hai.

Q: Kaunse voices available hain?

A: Available voices aapke operating system aur browser par depend karte hain. English (US, UK, Australian), Hindi, Spanish, French aur bahut si languages available hoti hain. Dropdown mein aapke system ke saare voices dikhenge.

Q: Kya Speech to Text offline kaam karta hai?

A: Speech to Text ke liye internet connection chahiye hota hai kyunki Chrome Google ke servers se recognition process karta hai. Text to Speech browser mein hi kaam karta hai aur offline bhi chalata hai.

Q: Kaunsa browser use karna chahiye?

A: Speech to Text ke liye Google Chrome ya Microsoft Edge best hai. Text to Speech sabhi modern browsers mein kaam karta hai.

Q: Kya main mobile pe use kar sakta hoon?

A: Haan! Dono tools mobile pe bhi kaam karte hain â€” Android Chrome aur iOS Safari mein. Voice availability device ke OS par depend karti hai.
