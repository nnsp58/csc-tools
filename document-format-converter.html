Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What document formats can I convert?

A: We support PDF, DOCX, TXT, HTML, and JSON formats. You can convert between any of these formats.

Q: Is there a file size limit?

A: For optimal performance, we recommend files under 10MB.

Q: Will formatting be preserved?

A: We try to maintain formatting, but complex layouts may not transfer perfectly between different formats.

Q: Are my documents secure?

A: Yes! All processing happens in your browser. No documents are uploaded to our servers.
