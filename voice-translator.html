Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is my microphone not working?

A: Please ensure you have granted microphone permissions in your browser. Look for the camera/mic icon in your address bar to manage permissions.

Q: Which languages are supported?

A: We support all major world languages and many Indian regional languages including Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and more.

Q: Does this work in all browsers?

A: Voice translation works best in modern browsers like Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Safari on iOS/macOS.
