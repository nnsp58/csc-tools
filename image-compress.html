Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does the target size option work?

A: When you specify a target size (e.g., 100KB), our engine performs multiple compression passes to get as close to that size as possible while maintaining the best possible clarity.

Q: Will my images look blurry?

A: At "Standard" compression, the difference is almost invisible to the human eye. We recommend "Minimal" if you need to keep perfect detail for printing.

Q: Is there a limit on how many images I can compress?

A: You can upload up to 20 images at a time. For best performance, we recommend batching large sets of high-resolution photos.
