Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is this password generator secure?

A: Yes! All passwords are generated locally in your browser using JavaScript. No passwords are sent to our servers.

Q: What is a strong password?

A: A strong password should be at least 12-16 characters long and include a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

Q: Should I reuse my passwords?

A: Never. Each of your accounts should have a unique, strong password. Using a password manager is highly recommended.
