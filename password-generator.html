Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are these passwords stored anywhere?

A: No. All password generation happens locally in your browser using the Cryptography API (if available) or Math.random. Your passwords never leave your computer.

Q: What makes a password strong?

A: A high-entropy password (length > 12 characters with mixed cases and symbols) is exponentially harder for hackers to crack using brute-force methods.

Q: How often should I change my passwords?

A: Security experts recommend using unique, strong passwords for every account and changing them if you suspect a breach. Using a password manager is the best way to stay secure.
