Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the conversion accurate for Hindi text?

A: Yes! Our tool is optimized to preserve Hindi characters and formatting during the extraction process.

Q: Can I convert scanned PDF to Word?

A: Currently, we support text extraction from digital PDFs. Scanned PDFs will be converted to high-quality images instead of editable text.

Q: Are my files stored on your server?

A: No. All processing happens in real-time and your files are not stored permanently. We prioritize your privacy.
